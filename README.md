# :rocket: Car Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project
This project features an end-to-end Car Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI. It provides deep insights into sales trends, pricing behavior, dealership performance, and customer purchasing patterns. The dashboard is divided into two interactive sections: Overview and Details, allowing users to explore data at both high and granular level.

### ⬇️ (KPIs) Requirements For Summary Dashboard:-

:sparkles: **Sales Overview** – We need to track overall car sales performance with:
- Year-to-Date (YTD) Total Sales  
- Month-to-Date (MTD) Total Sales  
- Year-over-Year (YOY) Growth in Total Sales  
- Difference between YTD Sales and Previous Year-to-Date (PTYD) Sales  

:sparkles: **Average Price Analysis** – To understand pricing trends across car models:
- YTD Average Price  
- MTD Average Price  
- YOY Growth in Average Price  
- Difference between YTD Average Price and PTYD Average Price  

:sparkles: **Cars Sold Metrics** – To measure volume and demand:
- YTD Cars Sold  
- MTD Cars Sold  
- YOY Growth in Cars Sold  
- Difference between YTD Cars Sold and PTYD Cars Sold

### ⬇️ (KPIs) Requirements For Overview Dashboard (Charts):-

:sparkles: **YTD Sales Weekly Trend (Line Chart)** –  
To display the weekly trend of YTD sales, with weeks on the X-axis and total sales amount on the Y-axis. This helps identify patterns and weekly performance swings.

:sparkles: **YTD Total Sales by Body Style (Pie Chart)** –  
To visualize the contribution of each car body style (SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, etc.) to total YTD sales.

:sparkles: **YTD Total Sales by Color (Pie Chart)** –  
To highlight which car colors are contributing the most to overall sales, helping understand customer preferences.

:sparkles: **YTD Cars Sold by Dealer Region (Map Chart)** –  
To show geographic distribution of sales volume by region, helping identify strong and weak-performing areas.

:sparkles: **Company-wise Sales Trend (Grid/Table)** –  
To provide a clear grid showcasing each car company alongside their YTD sales performance for comparison.

:sparkles: **Detailed Car Sales Grid (Table)** –  
To list all relevant transaction-level details such as car model, body style, color, sales amount, dealer region, and sale date for deep-dive analysis and validation.
